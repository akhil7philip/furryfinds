# About Furry Finds

## Who We Are

Furry Finds started because we were tired of buying "indestructible" dog toys that lasted 20 minutes and "cozy" pet beds that our cats ignored completely.

We're a group of pet parents — dog people, cat people, the occasional rabbit person — who got together to actually test the stuff we buy for our animals. Not in a lab. In our homes, with our actual pets, in the chaos of real life.

## How We Test

**Real pets, real mess.** Our test crew includes:
- A 75-lb golden retriever who thinks every toy is a demolition project
- A senior beagle with arthritis who needs orthopedic support
- Two cats who sleep on everything except the $80 cat bed
- A high-energy border collie who goes through leashes like toilet paper
- A chihuahua who proves that small dogs are not gentle dogs

**We use the products for weeks, not hours.** A 10-minute unboxing video tells you nothing. We live with these products:
- Chew toys get 2+ weeks of daily abuse
- Beds are tested for washability (multiple cycles, because pets are messy)
- Harnesses go on actual walks in rain, mud, and that weird humidity that makes everything smell
- Food bowls get the "left in the sink overnight" test

**We check the boring stuff.** Safety certifications, recall history, material sourcing, whether that "organic" label actually means anything. We read the fine print so you don't have to.

## Our Review Policy

- **No sponsored posts.** If a brand sent it to us for free, we say so — but most of the time, we bought it ourselves.
- **Honest about durability.** We tell you when something falls apart. We also tell you when it survives longer than expected.
- **Breed matters.** A chew toy that's perfect for a 10-lb terrier will be a choking hazard for a Great Dane. We specify who each product is actually for.
- **Vet-checked when possible.** We run safety and health claims past a veterinarian before publishing. (Dr. Sarah, our consulting vet, is very patient with our questions.)
