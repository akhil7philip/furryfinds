## Safety & Maintenance Notes

- **Clean the bowl weekly.** Bacteria build up in food residue. Stainless steel bowls (Granary, WOPET) are dishwasher-safe. Plastic bowls should be hand-washed with hot, soapy water.
- **Check battery levels monthly.** Even feeders with AC adapters need battery backup. Mark your calendar to check battery levels on the first of each month.
- **Kibble size matters.** Every feeder has a maximum kibble size. Check your pet food's kibble dimensions before buying. Large-breed dog food often exceeds 15mm — too big for cat-focused feeders.
- **Do not rely solely on automation for pets with medical conditions.** Diabetic pets, pets on timed medication, or pets with eating disorders need human supervision. Automatic feeders are a convenience, not a replacement for care.
- **Test for 3 days before traveling.** Never leave a new feeder untested on a long trip. Run it for 3 days at home, verify portions, and confirm your pet is comfortable with the dispensing noise.
