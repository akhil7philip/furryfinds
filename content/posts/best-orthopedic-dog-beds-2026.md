## How to Choose the Right Size

Measure your dog from nose to base of tail while sleeping in their preferred position — stretched out, curled, or on their side:

| Dog Size | Bed Size (Length) | Example Breeds |
|----------|------------------|----------------|
| Small (under 25 lbs) | 30" | Dachshund, Shih Tzu, Pug |
| Medium (25-50 lbs) | 36" | Beagle, Border Collie, Cocker Spaniel |
| Large (50-80 lbs) | 42" | Golden Retriever, Labrador, Boxer |
| Giant (80+ lbs) | 48"+ | Great Dane, Mastiff, Bernese Mountain Dog |

**Rule:** Buy one size larger than the measurement. Dogs shift in sleep and need extra space.

## Care and Maintenance

- **Wash covers weekly** — dogs carry dirt, pollen, and bacteria from outside. Unwashed covers become allergen traps.
- **Spot-clean foam monthly** — vacuum surface debris, spot-clean with enzyme cleaner for accidents.
- **Rotate the bed** — flip monthly to distribute wear evenly.
- **Replace when** — foam no longer rebounds within 24 hours, cover tears expose foam, or dog avoids the bed.
