## Smart Feeder Features Explained

### WiFi: 2.4GHz vs. 5GHz

Most smart feeders use 2.4GHz WiFi — it has longer range and better wall penetration than 5GHz. The downside: 2.4GHz networks are often congested with dozens of devices, causing slower reconnects. The PETKIT YumShare is the only feeder we tested with 5GHz support, which reconnects faster on modern mesh networks.

**Our recommendation:** If your router supports band steering (most mesh systems do), a 2.4GHz feeder is fine. If you have a 5GHz-only network or live in a congested apartment building, the PETKIT's dual-band support is worth the extra $16.

### Portion Accuracy: Portion-Based vs. Weight-Based

Every feeder except the Pawsync uses portion-based dispensing — "1 portion = approximately 5 grams." The actual weight varies based on kibble size, shape, and density. We measured 3–5g variation per portion across all portion-based feeders.

The Pawsync uses a built-in scale and dispenses by exact gram weight. If your pet needs 25g per meal, you get 25g — not "approximately 25g." For weight management, diabetes, or any medical diet, weight-based is genuinely better.

### Camera Quality: What 1080p Actually Means

All three camera feeders we tested claim "1080p," but quality varies significantly:

- **PETLIBRO Space:** Best low-light performance, widest angle (145°), clearest night vision
- **WOPET 6L:** Good daylight quality, grainy night vision, narrower angle (130°)
- **PETKIT YumShare:** No camera in the feeder itself, but the AI facial recognition camera is for pet ID, not monitoring

If camera quality is your priority, the PETLIBRO Space justifies its $149 price. If you just need to see if the bowl is empty, the WOPET is fine.

### Power Outage Behavior

We unplugged every feeder and monitored what happened:

| Feeder | Switches to Battery? | App Notification? | Battery Life |
|--------|---------------------|-------------------|--------------|
| Petlibro Granary | Yes | Yes (30 sec) | 7+ days |
| PETKIT YumShare | Yes | Yes (45 sec) | 3 days |
| Pawsync 6L | Yes | Yes (60 sec) | 6 days |
| WOPET 6L Camera | Yes | No | 4 days |
| PETLIBRO Space | Yes | Yes (30 sec) | 5 days |
| PitPet Smart | Yes | No | 3 days |

**Critical finding:** The WOPET 6L and PitPet switched to battery but did not send app notifications. If you are traveling and a power outage happens, you will not know unless you check manually. The Petlibro, PETKIT, and Pawsync all notify you within a minute.
